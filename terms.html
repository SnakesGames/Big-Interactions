The Game

Everything you can do in Gorilla Simulator X.

How it plays

You move like a gorilla: push off the ground to run, grab walls to climb, and punch blocks to break them. Every block you break pays coins, and your pets help.

100+

Pets to collect

Cube pets from Common to Mythic, plus giant Huge pets. Any hatch can come out Golden.

9

Zones to unlock

Meadow, Forest, Beach, Desert, Snow, Volcano, Candy, Crystal and Cosmic. Each one pays more coins than the last.

x3

Fuse and upgrade

Fuse three of a pet into a stronger one, then turn your best pets Golden and Rainbow.

Rebirth

Start over stronger

Rebirth for permanent boosts, buy upgrades and climb the ranks.

Trade

Play together

Join a public room or make a private room code for your friends, and trade pets with other players.

Live

Events

Double coins, lucky hours, pet parties, boss fights and limited-time worlds.

Halloween Event

Step through the portal in the hub to reach a haunted island. Break pumpkins, candy buckets and gravestones to collect Candy, then spend it on the Spooky Egg and the Haunted Egg.

The Haunted Egg holds 10 exclusive pets, including the Huge Pumpkin King, Huge Grim Reaper and Huge Spectral Ghost. They can only be hatched while the event is live.
